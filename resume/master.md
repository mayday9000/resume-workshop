# Master Inventory

The uncut record of every role, project and accomplishment. No length limit. Nothing is cut here.
The curated one-pagers in `resume/` and `resume/variants/` are built *from* this file.

Anything marked `[[confirm: ...]]` is unverified and must not reach a curated page until the
candidate confirms it. `npm run verify --strict` fails while any marker remains, which is what stops
a guess from shipping.

Replace everything below with your own history. Keep far more detail here than any single page can
hold; that surplus is what makes tailoring to a specific posting possible.
